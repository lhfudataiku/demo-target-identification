# Decision log — Part 2, the target prioritizer

Append-only. Every entry is dated and states what was decided **and why**, including the decisions that
were reversed. Extracted from `TARGET_PRIORITIZER.md` on 2026-08-19 — it was 82 lines holding 312
numbers, which is reference material, not reading material.

**Read this when you need to know why something is the way it is.** For the method itself see
[TARGET_PRIORITIZER.md](TARGET_PRIORITIZER.md); for the demo see
[DEMO_NARRATIVE.md](DEMO_NARRATIVE.md).

**Corrections are entries, not edits.** Where a later measurement overturned an earlier claim, both
entries stay and the later one says so. That record is the most useful thing in this file.
