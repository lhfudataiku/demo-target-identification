Act 3 & Act 4 · panel selection

Which families and diseases the demo should carry

Two decisions, taken against measured evidence rather than clinical intuition: which disease families Act 3 can support, and which individual diseases Act 4 should shortlist. Both were tested on the bar that matters for a scientific review — do the top-ranked genes match what the field already knows.

The constraint that decides Act 3

Subtype structure exists only in oncology

Act 3 needs families whose subtypes can each be scored, overlapped, and split into a common programme versus something specific. Across all 670 validation diseases, only oncology has families shaped that way.

therapeutic area diseases ≥50 positives families with ≥2 terms
Oncology24614410+ well-formed
Autoimmune / inflammatory23121 — asthma, 3 terms
Cardio / renal / metabolic32172, both unusable

Autoimmune and CVRM diseases are present with usable labels — but as singletons, each its own family. They have no subtypes to score. Atherosclerosis, the only CVRM family with three terms, carries 40 positives in total.

This splits the two decisions. Act 3 must be oncology. Therapeutic-area breadth has to come from Act 4, where individual diseases stand on their own.

Include
Breast cancer — the molecular axis HER2+ · luminal A · luminal B · triple-negative  |  mean overlap 40% · common core 7 genes

The sharpest contrast available. Triple-negative shares only 14–19% of its top 50 with any other subtype, and the core common to all of them is just seven genes — so "what is subtype-specific" has real content. Use the molecular subtypes only: the histology terms (ductal, lobular) are 82–85% duplicates of HER2+ and add nothing.

Include
Uterine cancer — the histology axis 8 of 8 terms usable · AUC 0.866–0.971 · mean overlap 49% · zero near-duplicates

The best-formed family in the set and never evaluated before now. Every pair differs by 45–56% — uniformly separable, with no redundant term to explain away. Its subtypes are the histologies that actually drive endometrial management: serous and clear cell are the high-risk arms, low-grade endometrioid is hormone-responsive.

Optional
Stomach cancer — a third family if wanted 6 of 7 usable · mean overlap 47% · ERBB2 #13 · FGFR2 #32

Clinically live: HER2 testing is standard in gastric adenocarcinoma and FGFR2b is in late-stage trials — both surface in the top 50. Held back only because two families already carry Act 3, and the Lauren split (intestinal vs signet-ring) is one near-duplicate pair.

Reject
Lung cancer — as a family adenocarcinoma vs squamous 89% · vs small cell 79% · 4 near-duplicate pairs of 6

The counter-intuitive result. Lung's histologies are the most treatment-decisive distinction in the panel — and the model gives them nearly the same list. There is no subtype-specific programme to show. Keep one lung term for Act 4; drop the family view.

Reject
Obesity — fails on both counts 2 terms · 1 usable · morbid obesity 41 positives, 0 hits in its top 50

Structurally it cannot support Act 3: two terms, one of them below the 50-positive floor and ranking nothing, so there is no overlap matrix and no common-versus-specific split. It fails Act 4 separately and more interestingly — see below.

Reject
Hematologic cancer mean overlap 59% · 5 near-duplicate pairs · surface-antigen blind spot

20 usable terms, but they are different diseases rather than subtypes of one, they overlap heavily, and modern haematology therapy targets cell-surface antigens — the one thing this model systematically misses (below).

Asked and answered

Obesity — why it comes out, in detail

Worth setting out properly, because obesity is the most topical metabolic area there is and dropping it needs a better reason than a middling AUC.

validated targetrankwhat it is
MC4R38knownsetmelanotide — approved

GLP1R526knownsemaglutide, liraglutide

MRAP21,592knownmonogenic obesity
FTO9,011knownstrongest common obesity locus

0 of 16 in the top 20; 1 in the top 50, out of a 13,126-gene pool. The two molecules that define modern obesity medicine sit past #500.

And the reason is visible in the list itself

top 12  — BBS4, CTNNB1, BBS7, BBS5, BBS9, INS, BRAF, BBS2, BBIP1, BBS1, FGFR1, GRM1

Seven of the top twelve, and nine of the top fifty, are Bardet-Biedl / BBSome genes — a rare ciliopathy in which obesity is one feature alongside retinal dystrophy, polydactyly and renal anomalies. Neither diabetes term has a single BBS gene in its top 50, so this is specific to obesity, not a general artefact.

The BBSome is a tightly interconnected protein complex, which is exactly what PPI-topology features reward. It is not wrong — setmelanotide is in fact approved for BBS-related obesity — but a scientist assessing an obesity programme would see a rare ciliopathy complex where they expect incretin biology. This is the module bias the project already documents, in its most legible form, and it is the honest reason obesity comes out.

A limitation worth volunteering

The model finds intracellular drivers, not surface antigens

Measured, median rank within the disease's own pool

plasma cell myeloma  — surface antigens 5,105  ·  intracellular drivers 24
acute lymphoblastic leukemia  — surface antigens 3,624  ·  intracellular drivers 13

Myeloma's three transformative immunotherapy targets are all buried: CD38 at #5105, BCMA at #5656, SLAMF7 at #6672. In ALL, CD19 sits at #823. Meanwhile TP53, KRAS and NRAS land in the top 20.

This is coherent rather than random: cell-surface antigens are not enriched in the protein-interaction and pathway topology the features are built from, whereas signalling hubs are. It is a genuine boundary of the method, it explains why haematology is excluded, and it is better said first than discovered by the audience.

To confirm

What we need decided

  • Act 3 — two families or three? Breast and uterine are recommended. Stomach is ready if a third adds value.
  • Act 4 — 11 diseases across three areas, replacing the current 13. Six oncology, two autoimmune, three CVRM.
  • Keep breast carcinoma? It is a 67% duplicate of HER2+, but the parent-versus-subtype gap is the evidence behind decision #82. A narrative call, not a numbers one.
  • Uterine and the two autoimmune terms are not built. They need the persona filter repointed and the ranking zone rebuilt before they can appear in the app.
  • Extend the drug benchmark to the subtypes. Five breast subtypes and lung adenocarcinoma have no approved-target data at all, so six of seven oncology picks rest on reconstruction alone.
