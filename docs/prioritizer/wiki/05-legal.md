## Evidence and Scientific-Use Warning

Rankings and SHAP explanations describe patterns learned from the project’s biomedical evidence and
validation design. They do not establish causal relationships, therapeutic efficacy, safety, or
fitness for a particular patient, disease, target, or drug programme.

The project is not a medical device, clinical decision-support system, diagnostic system, or source
of treatment recommendations. It must not be used to make clinical, regulatory, reimbursement, or
patient-care decisions.

## AI and Model-Output Warning

Model rankings, filtering results, graph paths, and AI-assisted explanations can be incomplete,
incorrect, or affected by changes in upstream sources and project configuration. SHAP explains the
model’s feature contribution; it does not prove an underlying biological mechanism. Users must
verify conclusions against authoritative evidence and expert scientific review before relying on
them.
