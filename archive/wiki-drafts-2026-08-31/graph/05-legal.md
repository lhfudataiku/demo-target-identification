# Legal Disclaimer on Demo Data

`DEMO_KG_LS` is a demonstration project built from public biomedical resources, including HGNC,
MONDO, Open Targets, Menche, HuRI, STRING, Reactome, Gene Ontology, NCBI gene annotations, and
HPO. The project does not establish ownership of these source materials or grant rights to
redistribute them, their derived datasets, or graph artifacts.

Each source has its own licensing, attribution, access, and redistribution terms. Anyone copying,
deploying, or distributing this POC must review the current terms for every source and retain any
required notices or attribution. Sources that require commercial licences, credentials, or other
restricted access were deliberately excluded from this reconstruction.

## Evidence and Scientific-Use Warning

Graph relationships represent integrated source assertions and identifiers. They do not establish
causality, therapeutic efficacy, safety, or fitness for a particular patient, disease, or drug
programme. Open Targets association scores are source-side prioritization heuristics, not curated
confidence measures. Approved and investigational drug-disease evidence is kept separate so that
the graph does not overstate clinical approval.

The project is not a scientific research, clinical decision-support system, or source of treatment 
recommendations. It must not be used to make clinical, regulatory, or patient-care decisions.

## Visual Graph and AI-Assistant Warning

Interactive exploration and AI-assisted graph queries are demonstration features. Responses and
visualizations can be incomplete, incorrect, or affected by changes in upstream public sources.
Users must verify conclusions against authoritative scientific sources and the relevant underlying
evidence before relying on them.

The project is provided as-is for demonstration and internal evaluation, without a warranty of
accuracy, completeness, availability, non-infringement, fitness for a particular purpose, or
production suitability.
